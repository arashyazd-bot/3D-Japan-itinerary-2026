Honeymoon Map Itinerary
========================

A 3D interactive map of the Arash & Laila Japan honeymoon trip
(April 17 – 30, 2026). Click any city to see the day-by-day plan.

----------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO OPEN THE APP
----------------------------------------------------------------

1. Double-click "Open Honeymoon Map.command"

2. The first time you open it, macOS may say:
       "Open Honeymoon Map.command can't be opened
        because it is from an unidentified developer."

   To fix this, do ONE of the following (only needed once):

   a) Right-click (or Control-click) on "Open Honeymoon Map.command"
      and choose "Open" from the menu. A dialog will appear — click
      "Open" again to confirm.

      OR

   b) Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, find
      the "Open Anyway" button next to the script name, and click it.

3. A Terminal window will open and your default web browser will
   launch the app at http://localhost:4173/

4. To stop the app: close the Terminal window (or press Control + C
   while it is focused).

----------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO USE THE APP
----------------------------------------------------------------

WELCOME SCREEN
   Click "START HONEYMOON" (or press Enter or Space) to enter.

THE 3D MAP
   - One finger drag (mouse: left-click + drag)
        → Pan around the map
   - Mouse wheel / two-finger pinch
        → Zoom in and out
   - Mouse: left-click + drag rotates the camera
     Touch: two-finger twist rotates the camera around the
     midpoint between your fingers (like Google Earth)
   - Right-click + drag pans on desktop

OPENING A CITY ITINERARY
   - Tap any glowing pillar on the map
        OR
   - Tap a city button at the bottom of the screen
        (Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka, Hakone, Tokyo Ginza)

INSIDE AN ITINERARY
   - The title and close (X) stay pinned at the top while you
     scroll through the day-by-day plan.
   - Click any event name to open it in Google Maps in a new tab.
   - Use the arrow buttons (← →) to move between cities, or just
     tap another city in the bottom dock.
   - Press Esc, click the X, or tap outside the menu to close.

AUDIO
   - Top-right "CODEC" button toggles ambient sound.
   - All music is synthesized live in your browser — no audio
     files are downloaded.

----------------------------------------------------------------
REQUIREMENTS
----------------------------------------------------------------

- macOS 10.15 or later
- A modern browser: Safari, Chrome, Firefox, or Edge
- Python 3 (comes with the Xcode Command Line Tools)
  If the launcher tells you Python is missing, open Terminal and run:
      xcode-select --install
  Then try again.
