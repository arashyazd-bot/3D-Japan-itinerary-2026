OPERATION: HONEYMOON  —  Japan 3D Map
Arash & Laila  ·  April 17 – 30, 2026

----------------------------------------------------------------------
TO START
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Double-click  "❤️ Start Honeymoon Map.command"
  2. A black Terminal window will open. Wait a second or two.
  3. Your browser will open automatically to the app.
  4. Click the "▸ START HONEYMOON" button to begin.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
IF macOS BLOCKS IT THE FIRST TIME
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  macOS may say  "cannot be opened because it is from an
  unidentified developer."  This is normal for files made
  outside the Mac App Store.

  Fix:  right-click (or Control-click) the .command file
        → choose "Open" → click "Open" again on the warning.

  You only need to do this once.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
TO STOP
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  Just close the Terminal window.  Or press Ctrl+C in it.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS INSIDE THE APP
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  • Drag                — rotate the camera
  • Scroll wheel        — zoom in / out  (your zoom is preserved)
  • Right-drag          — pan
  • Click a city pillar — open that stop's day-by-day briefing
  • ← / →               — cycle stops
  • Esc                 — close briefing
  • Click an event name — opens it in Google Maps
  • ♪ CODEC ON/OFF      — toggle music + SFX

----------------------------------------------------------------------
REQUIREMENTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  • macOS 13 (Ventura) or newer  (Python 3 is preinstalled)
  • A modern browser  (Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Arc)

Made for Arash & Laila.
